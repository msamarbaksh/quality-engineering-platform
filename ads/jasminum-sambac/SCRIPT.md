# "The Garden Follows You" — 30s Perfume Ad

**Product:** Chloé Atelier des Fleurs — Jasminum Sambac Eau de Parfum
**Entry:** OpenArt AD Award (30 seconds, 16:9, 1080p)
**Tagline:** *"Jasminum Sambac. The jasmine from your garden — wherever you go."*

## Look & feel
- Warm, soft, filmic: 35mm grain, golden highlights, creamy shallow depth of field.
- Color arc: warm amber (party) → soft ivory morning light (apartment) → lush green and white (jasmine dimension) → New York steel-grey that the green gradually takes over.
- Music: a solo piano motif that grows into strings and light chimes when the jasmine dimension opens. SFX: ribbon pulled, paper rustling, one perfume spritz, a soft "whoosh" of petals, birdsong mixing in with city noise.

## Characters
- **Yasmin** (late 20s): long dark wavy hair, warm olive skin, cream silk blouse and camel trousers for work.
- **Her sister** (early 30s): shoulder-length curly hair, sage-green party dress.

## Production pipeline (OpenArt)
1. **Reference images** (Nano Banana Pro): Yasmin character sheet, sister character sheet, perfume bottle hero shot.
2. **Video** (Seedance 2.5, element2video, 1080p): generated as three clips so each part stays controlled and consistent:
   - Clip A, 0:00–0:09: gift and reveal (shots 1–3)
   - Clip B, 0:09–0:16: morning spray and the jasmine dimension opening (shots 4–5)
   - Clip C, 0:16–0:30: New York walk, strangers entering the garden, product hero (shots 6–8)
3. **Edit:** join A+B+C, add the end card with the VO line, then balance the music.

## Note on the brand
Chloé is a real, trademarked brand. The contest brief says "any fictional brand, product, service or idea", so check the official rules before submitting. If using the real brand is a problem, a fictional stand-in works with the same script, e.g. **"Maison Yasmin — Jasminum Sambac"**.
